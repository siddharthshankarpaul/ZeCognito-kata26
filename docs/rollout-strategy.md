## Estate-Wide Rollout Strategy

[footfall_and_popularity/002](adrs/footfall_and_popularity/002-adr-phased-rollout-crowd-and-popularity-analytics.md) already gates its own three steps on the estate's visitor-volume curve, not a calendar date, since a date can arrive before the data needed to validate the next step exists. This page applies the same rule estate-wide: it sequences every platform and sub-problem ADR against [platform/ADR-007](adrs/platform/ADR-007-cost-model-and-investment-strategy.md)'s CapEx/OpEx, so the cost model has the time dimension it doesn't have on its own. No new decisions are made here, this is an ordering of decisions that already exist.

| Phase | Volume gate | What ships | Funded by (ADR-007) | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0: Foundation** | Day one, ~5,000/day | MQTT store-and-forward backbone, edge anonymiser, edge-vs-cloud CV placement, offline ticket signing, the two-plane safety model, Augur (LLM gateway), guardrails and evals, production monitoring, funded gate connectivity | MQTT sensors, edge cluster nodes, estate gateway, gate connectivity, installation labour | Nothing, this is the estate opening its doors at all |
| **1: Ground truth** | ~5,000/day and up, concurrent with Phase 0 | Footfall Step 1 (beam+CV counters, per-zone forecast, Queue Management Agent), animal welfare Wave 1 (feed/health sensors, edge CV, piranha edge vision), the Profitability Advisor running on aggregate signals only, consent-gated Guide recommendations | Piranha house GPU box + cameras, Augur's baseline LLM spend | Phase 0's edge backbone and Augur gateway |
| **2: Cross-zone prediction & calibration** | ~8,000 to 10,000/day | Footfall Step 2 (learned transition graph, `simulate_scenario` tool), the animal-welfare calibration investment: keeper/vet annotation labour, a governed recalibration cadence | Keeper and vet annotation labour line | A funding decision on calibration labour, per [README §16](../README.md#16-assumptions); Step 2's model needs Step 1's ticket-scan history to train on |
| **3: Platform reuse & re-architecture watch** | ~12,000 to 15,000/day | Footfall Step 3 (stereo-vision upgrade where crush density is proven, `simulate_scenario` reused by Pricing via MCP); a checkpoint on whether Admissions' modular monolith and the service-based core still fit | No new CapEx line committed yet, named as a roadmap intention | Step 2's backtest earning trust; the estate actually approaching the 15,000/day target [ADR-004](adrs/platform/ADR-004-architecture-style.md) is designed around |

**Why volume, not a calendar:** a date fixed today can arrive before Phase 2's training data exists, or long after the estate has already outgrown Phase 1. Gating on the estate's own growth curve means each phase is pulled into existence by evidence, not a plan drawn up in advance of it, the same reasoning [footfall_and_popularity/002](adrs/footfall_and_popularity/002-adr-phased-rollout-crowd-and-popularity-analytics.md) already applies to its own three steps.

**What this page is not:** a committed week-by-week schedule with dates or a fixed budget per phase. The estate doesn't have a calendar to commit to yet, and [platform/ADR-007](adrs/platform/ADR-007-cost-model-and-investment-strategy.md) already flags every unit cost as illustrative. What's fixed here is the order and the dependency, not the date.
